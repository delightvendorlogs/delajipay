QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

How do I fund my wallet?

Go to Fund wallet, enter an amount of ₦100 or more, and pay securely with Paystack.

What if my purchase fails?

Failed orders are refunded to your wallet automatically. If an order is still processing, please do not pay again; check History in a few minutes.

Where can I see my receipts?

Every order appears in your History with its status and details.

How do I contact support?

Open Help & Support in your dashboard to chat with our team, or email delajipay@gmail.com.
