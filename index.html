GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Some details depend on number availability and the services supported by our providers.

What is Sparkle SMS?＋

Sparkle SMS is a platform for exploring supported virtual numbers and viewing incoming SMS messages in one account.

Which countries and platforms are available?＋

Available destinations and supported platforms can change based on provider coverage. Sign in to view current options.

Are numbers rented?＋

Sparkle SMS is being designed around standard number purchases, not recurring rental subscriptions.

Will every number receive messages?＋

Message delivery depends on the selected number, platform and provider. Review the availability details before placing an order.
