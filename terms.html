Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Draft for review before launch.

1. About DelajiPay

DelajiPay is being developed as a digital platform for selected everyday payment services, including airtime, mobile data, electricity and television subscriptions. The platform is not a bank and does not represent that it is a licensed deposit-taking institution.

2. Account responsibilities

You must provide accurate information, keep your sign-in credentials private and promptly report suspected unauthorised access. You are responsible for checking recipient, meter, decoder and service details before confirming an order.

3. Payments and wallet

Any wallet feature will be governed by the final payment, funding, refund and withdrawal rules shown before the feature is activated. A displayed balance is not a bank deposit. Wallet credits and debits will be recorded and reconciled against verified payment and service-provider events.

4. Service availability

Service availability, pricing, fulfilment times and supported providers may vary. An order may be pending while a provider confirms its result. We will not describe a transaction as successful until its status is verified.

5. Errors, refunds and support

If a payment or service order appears incorrect, contact support with the transaction reference. Refund eligibility and timing will depend on the verified transaction result, applicable provider rules and law.

6. Acceptable use

You must not misuse the platform, attempt unauthorised access, submit fraudulent information or interfere with its operation.

7. Changes and contact

These draft terms may be updated before launch. The final operator identity, contact details, dispute process and governing-law provisions must be completed and reviewed before public transactions are enabled.
