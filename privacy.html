YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how DelajiPay handles personal information when you visit the website, create an account, contact support or use an available service. We aim to handle information fairly and securely in accordance with applicable Nigerian data-protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include your name, email address, phone number, account identifiers, authentication and security records, transaction references, payment status, service recipient details (such as a mobile number, meter or decoder number), support messages and information you choose to provide. Technical information may include device and browser details, IP address, access logs and essential session data.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, process requested payments and services, display transaction history, provide support, investigate errors, prevent fraud, secure and improve the platform, and meet legal obligations.

4. Lawful grounds

Where required by law, processing is based on an applicable lawful ground, which may include performing a contract or steps requested before a contract, complying with a legal obligation, legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, or your consent where required.

5. Sharing information

We may share information reasonably needed with payment gateways, bill-payment providers, hosting and infrastructure providers, security services, and professional or legal advisers. They may process information to complete transactions, operate the platform, protect users or meet legal requirements. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Payment security

Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers under their own privacy and security terms. Never send card PINs, online banking passwords or one-time passwords to DelajiPay support. We do not intend to request or store your full banking password or card PIN.

7. Storage and security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, transaction records, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

8. Transfers and cookies

Some service providers may process information outside Nigeria. Where applicable, safeguards required by data-protection law should apply. The website may use essential cookies or browser storage to keep you signed in, protect sessions and support core functions. Disabling essential storage may affect account features.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; this does not affect processing already lawfully carried out. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children, updates and contact

DelajiPay is not intended for children who are not legally able to use the service. We may update this policy as the platform develops; material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the Help and Support page on the DelajiPay website.
