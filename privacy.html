Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Draft for review before launch.

Information we may collect

When account features are enabled, DelajiPay may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, account identifiers, service recipient details, transaction references and support messages.

How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, process requested services, maintain transaction records, prevent fraud, provide customer support, meet legal obligations and improve the platform.

Payments and service partners

Payment gateways and bill-payment providers may receive the information needed to complete and verify a transaction. Their own privacy notices and terms may also apply. DelajiPay should not collect or store full card details directly.

Security and retention

We are designing access controls, database row-level security, audit records and secure server-side integrations. Information will be retained only as needed for service delivery, legal obligations, dispute handling and legitimate business purposes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. A final contact channel and procedure will be published before launch.

Cookies and updates

The production website may use essential storage for sign-in sessions and security. This policy may be updated, with the effective date shown above.

This is a draft. The final data controller identity, lawful bases, retention periods, processors, user-rights contact and Nigeria Data Protection Act compliance details must be completed before launch.
