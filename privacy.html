YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Sparkle SMS handles personal information when you visit the website, create an account, contact support or use virtual-number services. We aim to handle information fairly and securely in accordance with applicable Nigerian data-protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include your name, email address, phone number, account identifiers, authentication and security records, wallet and transaction references, selected country and service, allocated number, order status, incoming SMS contents or codes, support messages and information you choose to provide. Technical information may include device and browser details, IP address, access logs and essential session data.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, display available number offers, submit and track orders, deliver incoming SMS to the correct account, process wallet payments and refunds, provide support, investigate errors, prevent fraud, secure and improve the platform, and meet legal obligations.

4. Lawful grounds

Where required by law, processing is based on an applicable lawful ground, which may include performing a contract or steps requested before a contract, complying with a legal obligation, legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, or your consent where required.

5. Sharing information with providers

To fulfil a number order and retrieve its incoming message, necessary order information may be shared with the selected virtual-number and SMS provider. We may also share information with payment gateways, hosting and infrastructure providers, security services, and professional or legal advisers where reasonably necessary. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. SMS and verification data

Incoming SMS may contain verification codes or other personal information. We display messages only within the account associated with the order and apply access controls intended to prevent other customers from viewing them. Do not use the service to receive messages you are not authorised to access. Message delivery involves third-party telecommunications and provider systems and is not end-to-end encrypted.

7. Payment security

Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers under their own privacy and security terms. We do not intend to request or store your full banking password, card PIN or payment-card security code. Never send these details or a verification code to Sparkle SMS support.

8. Storage and security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, message and transaction history, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Provider retention and number reassignment practices may also apply.

9. Transfers and cookies

Our providers may process information outside Nigeria. Where applicable, safeguards required by data-protection law should apply. The website may use essential cookies or browser storage to keep you signed in, protect sessions and support core functions. Disabling essential storage may affect account features.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; this does not affect processing already lawfully carried out. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Children, updates and contact

Sparkle SMS is not intended for children who are not legally able to use the service. We may update this policy as the platform develops; material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact delajipay@gmail.com.
